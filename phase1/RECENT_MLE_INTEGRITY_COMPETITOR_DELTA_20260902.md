## Material delta

Two primary-source papers were absent from the current manuscript packet:

1. **DeltaML-Bench** ([arXiv:2608.19653v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19653v1),
   submitted 2026-08-20) evaluates agents on 48 real research repositories and
   combines improvement-over-baseline tasks with layered specification-gaming
   checks. It is a direct MLE benchmark and integrity precedent.
2. **BAITBENCH** ([arXiv:2608.30724v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30724v1),
   submitted 2026-08-31) builds three synthetic ML task families with planted
   shortcuts, recomputes performance on a hidden robust split, and packages run
   evidence for a two-judge reward-hacking protocol. It is a direct ML-agent
   evaluation-integrity precedent.

These works close broad claims such as “first trustworthy MLE-agent benchmark,”
“first hidden held-out ML-agent evaluation,” “first specification-gaming audit,”
and “first benchmark over real research repositories.” They should be cited as
strong motivation for integrity controls, not treated as generic background.

## What remains different

cross-family predictor study its primary released/evaluated unit. The current
Decision Corpus position remains narrower: provenance-bound sibling fragments,
physical-run/config/time isolation, incomplete-choice and pair-dependence auditing,
initialization/query/execution accounting, and one-shot outcome-blind temporal
closure.
