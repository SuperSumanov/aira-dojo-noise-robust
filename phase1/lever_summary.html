Poor-compute LLM ML-engineering search · what actually helps

One lever, two non-levers: under poor compute, clean evaluation is the lever — cheap critics and multi-fidelity are not.

Across a single-GPU study on real, externally-graded LLM solutions, two methodologically unrelated "cheap tricks" carry genuine signal yet fail the same way — the payoff never survives the regime you can afford. The one thing that moves the deployed solution is evaluation quality.

The lever
Evaluation quality

Clean full-fidelity grading deploys the best solution; the cheap selection statistic (single / mean / variance-penalty) does not matter.

Full-fidelity regret 0.009 vs ~0.03 for every 10% proxy. Fidelity is the knob — not how you select.

Non-lever I
A cheap learned critic

The frozen probe carries grade signal independent of self-report (H1) — but as a deployment veto the win is a random-pool artifact.

Random pools: Δregret +0.18. Real search trees: +0.007, not significant (95% CI crosses 0). rank ≠ regret.

Non-lever II
Multi-fidelity selection

Cheap low-fidelity screening only helps where candidate grades are dispersed — i.e. exactly the tasks poor compute cannot afford.

Affordable tasks are already near-oracle (headroom → 0); the only task with room (tps +0.044) has 3.6M-row training — unsearchable on one GPU.

The through-line — rank ≠ regret. Genuine rank skill (the probe's ablation-verified independence) fails to buy search utility across three framings: best-of-K selection, wrong-pair rescue, and the online veto. Necessary, not sufficient.
The method — falsify offline before you build. Four seconds-to-minutes offline probes each dissolved a "positive-looking" result before an expensive online build: best-of-K, random-vs-real-tree veto, bootstrap significance, multi-fidelity headroom.

Sharpens aira-dojo (search strategy barely matters) and pins the mechanism to evaluation noise (AIRA_2). Single RTX 3090 · DeepSeek backbone, never finetuned · external pristine grading, no LLM-as-judge · 289 graded solutions, 3 tabular tasks. Numbers: T1 §4, veto §5.4, headroom §6 of the draft.
